## Broader facts for the deep-dive (all ⚠)

1. **UCI 65 mm rim-depth cap (from 1 January 2026).** Mass-start road events now ban rims deeper than 65 mm. The UCI cited crosswind stability. Enve redesigned the SES 6.7 because its old 66 mm rear rim was 1 mm too deep, and the new one is 59/65. Swiss Side said in an open letter that tyre width and frame geometry matter more for steering than rim depth. AusCycling exempts non-UCI domestic events. Velo notes that WorldTour road-stage wheels average about 50 mm deep. Sources: https://www.bikeradar.com/news/uci-tech-rules-2026 ; https://velo.outsideonline.com/road/road-racing/uci-tech-restrictions-2026/ ; https://www.tour-magazin.de/en/professional-cycling/latest-news/open-letter-against-planned-uci-rules-for-wheels/ ; https://auscycling.org.au/news/how-new-uci-equipment-regulations-will-be-implemented-in-auscycling-technical-regulations ; https://www.bikeradar.com/news/enve-ses-6-7-pro-wheels-tadej-pogacar
2. **Hookless pressure ceiling.** ETRTO guidance caps hookless (TSS) rims at 5 bar / 72.5 psi, and tyres must pass a retention test above 110% of that. Zipp's 353/454 NSW are rated at about 72–73 psi. Hooked flagships (Enve SES 6.7 Pro, 4.5 Pro) are rated to 100 psi. Several 2026 flagships use hooked rims (Enve 6.7 Pro, DT ARC 2025, Roval CLX III, Princeton, Scope Artech 4), while Zipp NSW and Cadex Max 50 are hookless. The industry has not converged on one approach. The cap comes only from secondary sources (Cycling Weekly, Goodyear spec sheet); I did not see the ETRTO manual itself. Sources: https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/hookless-controversy-why-jakobsens-crash-at-the-uae-tour-has-opened-yet-another-front-in-the-rim-safety-debate/ ; https://www.slowtwitch.com/cycling/hookless-rim-and-tire-compatibility-update/ ; https://roadbikeaction.com/zipp-454-nsw-wheel-review/ ; https://bikerumor.com/enves-new-ses-6-7-pro-wheelset/
3. **Wider rims are now standard.** Internal widths of 21–25 mm have replaced the 17–19 mm of a few years ago. Fulcrum went from 19 to 23 mm, Vision from 19 to 21 mm, and Princeton from 18.5 (2021) to 21 to 22 mm (EVO II). Most brands now tune for 28 mm tyres (Enve 6.7 Pro, Cadex Max 40), and Zipp's 353 is sized for 30 mm.
