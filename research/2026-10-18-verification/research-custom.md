### "Titanium revival": sourced trend facts (all ⚠)

1. **Bigger brands moving into Ti.** In 2026 Eddy Merckx added titanium versions of the Corsa Pévèle and Corsa Strasbourg (Columbus Hyperion tubing, handmade in Italy), at £4,499 / €4,999 per frameset. This followed a Ti gravel prototype shown at the UCI Gravel Worlds.
   - https://www.eddymerckx.com/news/article/148329
   - https://www.bikeradar.com/news/eddy-merckx-prototype-titanium-gravel-bike
2. **BikeRadar ran a "5 best new titanium gravel bikes of 2026 so far" round-up.** The search summary says it notes new road bikes from Enigma, Passoni and J.Laverack, and it includes the Baum DBM 32. Not opened.
   - https://www.bikeradar.com/features/tech/2026-s-best-new-titanium-gravel-bikes-so-far
3. **Ti printing is going mainstream among small builders.** Examples: J.Laverack AM64, Sturdy's printed junctions (Cilla, SC-G, Eimar TT), Firefly's printed fork crowns and dropouts, and No.22's 3D-printed Reactor Aero. Urwahn also has a limited-edition Softride Ti64 frame (mid-2026), but its printing details were not verified.
   - Sources: as in each builder entry above
   - Urwahn: https://www.tour-magazin.de/en/frame/urwahn-softride-ti64-limited-edition-bike-frame-made-from-aerospace-material/
4. **Counterpoint: the revival has casualties.** Lynskey (Chattanooga, USA) filed for Chapter 11 on **30 April 2026**. Reports appeared in May. Its filing cited raw titanium costs up about 40% since 2021, plus Shopify chargebacks and reserves of about US$550k. A May 19 report said it owed FirstBank about US$1.81m.
   - https://bicycleretailer.com/industry-news/2026/05/06/lynskey-performance-products-files-chapter-11
   - https://bicycleretailer.com/industry-news/2026/05/19/documents-lynskey-owes-bank-18-million
   - https://escapecollective.com/lynskey-bicycles-has-filed-for-chapter-11-bankruptcy/
   - https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/american-titanium-bike-brand-lynskey-files-for-bankruptcy-protection-frames-offered-at-significant-discount
   - **[Good]**: several outlets agree.
5. **Atherton Bikes fire.** On Sunday **11 October 2026** a fire broke out at Atherton Bikes' HQ and factory in Machynlleth, Wales. No one was injured. Atherton expects disruption to production and deliveries, and says dispatch dates should be treated as unconfirmed. The cause and the extent of the damage have not been reported.
   - https://www.bikeradar.com/news/firefighters-tackle-heartbreaking-blaze-at-atherton-factory-in-wales
   - https://www.itv.com/news/wales/2026-10-11/fire-in-machynlleth-devastates-world-champions-bike-factory
   - https://singletrackworld.com/2026/10/atherton-bikes-factory-fire/
   - **[Good]** on the date. Atherton is known for 3D-printed Ti lugs, but that was not re-verified in this session.
6. **Custom builders are adding stock or batch lines** to cut lead times. Examples: Mosaic Batch Built (launched 2023, Level 1 added 2026), Sturdy SC-G off-the-peg, Stinner Podium stock frames.
   - Sources: as in each builder entry above
