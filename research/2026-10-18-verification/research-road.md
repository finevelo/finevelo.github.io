## Read this first: how far these figures can be trusted

- **None of the source pages could be opened.** WebFetch and curl failed for every host tried: road.cc, cyclingnews.com, specialized.com and tour-magazin.de (DNS failure / egress proxy 403). So **every figure below comes from search-engine result summaries, not from reading the page.** Under the brief's rule, **every figure is flagged ⚠**. Before print, open each source URL and check the figure against it.
- **The session's web-search limit (200 calls, shared across agents) ran out partway through.** Not researched as a result: item 10 (other 2025–26 flagships), Cervélo R5 GBP/EUR prices and launch date, and exact launch dates for several bikes. Gaps are marked **NOT VERIFIED**.
- "Claimed" means a manufacturer figure (sometimes repeated by an outlet or retailer). "Measured" means an independent scale, and the outlet is named.

## HEADLINE CORRECTION: the Tarmac SL8 has been replaced

**The Specialized S-Works Tarmac SL9 launched on or around 30 June 2026, just before the 2026 Tour de France. The SL8 is now the outgoing model, being sold off at discount.** A "Tarmac SL8 vs Dogma F" article should be reframed as **Tarmac SL9 vs Dogma F**, or at least acknowledge the SL9. ⚠
- Cyclingnews (summer 2026): "The Specialized S-Works Tarmac SL9 may be getting all the early Tour de France headlines but its outgoing Tarmac SL8 sibling is carrying up to 16 percent off". https://www.cyclingnews.com/cycling-tech-components/the-specialized-s-works-tarmac-sl9-maybe-getting-all-the-early-tour-de-france-headlines-but-its-outgoing-tarmac-sl8-sibling-is-carrying-up-to-16-percent-off/ ⚠
- road.cc launch story: "The fastest road bike ever made? Specialized unveils the S-Works Tarmac SL9". https://road.cc/tech-news/the-fastest-road-bike-ever-made-specialized-unveils-the-s-works-tarmac-sl9 ⚠
- Launch-date conflict: one outlet gives 30 June 2026. Escape Collective's news piece is dated 30 June 2026. Spanish trade site Interempresas is dated 2 July 2026. Several say "days before the Tour". Exact embargo date still needs checking against Specialized's newsroom. ⚠
- The rest of the range (S-Level, Expert, Comp) followed later. One retailer blog says the full lineup was available from 25 Aug 2026. ⚠

## Pro teams on Specialized in 2026, and notable wins

- **Red Bull–BORA–hansgrohe** ride the Tarmac SL9 at the 2026 Tour (everything except the time trials), with Remco Evenepoel and Florian Lipowitz as co-leaders ⚠ (Red Bull / search summary).
- **Soudal Quick-Step** remain on Specialized through 2026. They sponsor since 2012, and the contract runs to end-2026. Reports from April 2026 (Escape Collective, Wielerflits) say the team **switches to Merida for 2027**. The team declined to comment ⚠ https://escapecollective.com/reports-soudal-quick-step-to-switch-from-specialized-to-merida-in-2027/
- 2026 results seen (bike model at each race not confirmed):
  - Tim Merlier (Soudal Quick-Step) won **Tour stage 12** ⚠ (sortiraparis.com).
  - Paul Magnier (SQS) won **3 Giro stages plus the points jersey** in May 2026, most likely on the SL8 because the SL9 was not yet launched ⚠ (rouleur.cc).
  - Evenepoel was 3rd on Tour stage 2 and 2nd on stage 20. **No Red Bull–Bora stage win was found** in the results seen ⚠ (cyclingstage.com; flobikes.com).
  - Pogačar (Colnago) won the 2026 Tour ⚠.
