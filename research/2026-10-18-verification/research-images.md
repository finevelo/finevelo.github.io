# Finevelo image candidates, 18 Oct 2026

I checked each licence on the Wikimedia Commons file page (via WebFetch) and looked at a 960 px preview of every recommended image.

**Access limits this session:**
- unsplash.com and pexels.com could not be reached (DNS and proxy refusal), so I could not verify any Unsplash or Pexels photo page. All candidates below come from Commons.
- The Pexels candidate in research/2026-10-18.md §4 (Robert So, ID 37134604) has **not** been re-verified in this session.

**Credit format:** "Author / Wikimedia Commons, Licence". For CC BY and CC BY-SA, also link the licence URL and the file page. CC BY-SA also requires that edited versions stay CC BY-SA.

**Uploader check:** "OK" means a private photographer, a museum or a university, not a manufacturer, team or agency.
