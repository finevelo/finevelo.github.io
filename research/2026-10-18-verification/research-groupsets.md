## 1. Shimano Dura-Ace Di2

### Current generation as of Oct 2026: still R9200. R9300 is not launched.
- **No official R9300 groupset launch found up to 18 Oct 2026.** ⚠
  - Prototype 13-speed groupsets appeared on Alpecin-Premier Tech bikes at stage 1 of the 2026 Vuelta ([Cyclingnews](https://www.cyclingnews.com/cycling-tech-components/groupsets/new-dura-ace-spotted/)). Mathieu van der Poel rode one at the Worlds in Montréal in late September ([Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/mathieu-van-der-poel-rides-13-speed-dura-ace-prototype-at-uci-world-championships)). ⚠
  - Cycling Weekly reportedly says a commercial release is unlikely before early 2027. Bike-Room reports Shimano told the press that nothing major launches in 2026 ([bike-room](https://bike-room.com/blogs/news/shimano-dura-ace-13-speed-used-bikes-explained); a retailer blog, not a core outlet). ⚠
  - Leaks suggest it stays semi-wireless, with alloy cranks and a redesigned shifter button ([the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/08/23/dura-ace-r9300-13-speed-spotted/), not a core outlet). Some speculative sources expect it to be fully wireless instead; the sources disagree. ⚠
  - A March 2026 E-Tube app update reportedly expanded the gear display to 13 positions ([biketips](https://biketips.com/shimano-13-speed-dura-ace-r9300-e-tube-leak-2026/), low-tier source). ⚠ A search of Shimano's own E-Tube notes found no such entry.
- **R9300-branded parts that *have* launched:** ⚠
  - **WH-R9370 wheels**, 29 July 2026, with carbon spokes. Up to 220 g saved per wheelset. C50 listed at 1,302 g, about $2,970 ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321970/20260729/shimano-dura-ace-wh-r9370-launches-carbon-spokes-slash-weight-signal-r9300-groupset-era.htm), not a core outlet). Racycles dates the wheel launch to September instead, a conflict.
  - **PD-R9300 pedals**: €249, 228 g per pair, reviewed 14 Oct 2026 ([3bikes](https://www.3bikes.fr/en/2026/10/14/review-of-the-new-shimano-dura-ace-pd-r9300-pedals-and-the-spd-slr-system/)). Only the claimed weight appears in the summary.
- **Possible lever revision:** E-Tube Project Pro 5.4.6 (1 Sep 2026) adds compatibility for ST-R9271, ST-R8171, ST-R9171 and ST-R8071 shifters (Shimano DE E-Tube page). A WeightWeenies thread describes ST-R9271 as an updated 12-speed lever that is wireless-only and has no SD300 wired option ([WW forum](https://weightweenies.starbike.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=2007241)). This is unverified and not reported by any outlet. ⚠ **Worth a question to Shimano.**
- **Estimated R9300 price** of about €5,000 / $4,899 comes from a retailer blog (rydecruz). It is speculation. Do not use.

### R9200 / R9270 (disc) facts ⚠
- **Launch:** 31 Aug 2021, alongside Ultegra R8100. 12-speed, electronic only. Disc levers are wireless; the rim-brake version is wired ([BikeRadar](https://bikeradar.com/news/shimano-dura-ace-di2-r9200)).
- **Architecture:** semi-wireless. Wireless shifters run on coin cells. The derailleurs are wired to a central internal battery.
- **Shifting claim:** 58% faster rear and 45% faster front than R9150 (Shimano claim, as repeated by thetriathlete.co.uk and retailers).
- **Gearing:** 50/34 and 52/36 chainrings; 11-30 and 11-34 cassettes (retailer listings). A 54/40 option is not confirmed in the results. Keeps the 11-speed freehub (Gravel Cyclist headline).
- **Weight:**
  - Claimed: 35 g heavier than R9170 (Shimano, via BikeRadar).
  - Claimed total: 2,507 g ([Velo gallery](https://velo.outsideonline.com/gallery/shimano-dura-ace-r9200-and-ultegra-r8100-photos-weights-and-prices/)). Whether a PM is included is not stated.
  - Measured by BikeRadar: 2,514 g ([BikeRadar news](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/new-sram-red-axs)).
  - Measured by BikeRadar: 2,579 g with 52/36 PM crank and 11-34 ([BikeRadar versus](https://www.bikeradar.com/features/tech/versus/shimano-dura-ace-di2-r9200-vs-sram-red-axs)).
  - BikeRadar measured the 11-34 cassette at 256 g.
  - Cycling Weekly gives 2,438.5 g without PM. It is unclear whether this was weighed or claimed ([CW review](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/reviews/shimano-dura-ace-r9200-12-speed-electronic-hydraulic-groupset-review)).
  - RD-R9250 claimed at 215 g ([BikeRadar](https://bikeradar.com/news/shimano-dura-ace-di2-r9200)).
- **Price at launch:**
  - UK: just under £3,700 for R9270 without PM ([BikeRadar](https://bikeradar.com/news/shimano-dura-ace-di2-r9200)).
  - EU: €3,899 for R9270 and €3,849 for R9250 rim. A PM adds about €1,100 ([BetterShifting](https://bettershifting.com/dura-ace-r9250-ultegra-r8150-wireless-di2/), not a core outlet).
  - US: no official MSRP found. Performance Bike lists about $4,223, and the crank may be excluded.
- **Battery:** no claimed range found.
- **Firmware:** no notable 2024–26 Dura-Ace feature update found. Updates go through E-Tube Project and the E-Tube Cyclist app over Bluetooth.

## 2. SRAM Red AXS (E1)

### Facts ⚠
- **Launch:** BikeRadar's launch story is dated 15 May 2024 ([BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/new-sram-red-axs)).
- **Speeds and architecture:** 12-speed, fully wireless AXS. Lever coin cell (CR2032) lasts about 2 years ([SRAM support](https://support.sram.com/hc/en-us/articles/27118806924827-How-long-do-the-RED-AXS-shift-brake-control-batteries-last)). No derailleur battery claim found.
- **Gearing:**
  - Chainrings 46/33, 48/35, 50/37, 52/39, 54/41, 56/43 (from a retailer's front derailleur compatibility list; [materiel-velo](https://www.materiel-velo.com/gb/1880791-sram-red-axs-e1-front-derailleur.html)). Needs SRAM confirmation, since some sizes may be aero or 1x only.
  - Cassettes 10-28, 10-30, 10-33, 10-36 ([BikeRadar review](https://www.bikeradar.com/reviews/components/groupsets/groupset-road/sram-red-axs-review)).
- **Features:**
  - New lever and hood, 83 g lighter than the previous lever.
  - Reach adjustment up to 15 mm via a 2.5 mm bolt; it is mechanical, not hydraulic.
  - Contact-point adjustment.
  - Programmable "bonus" button that can control ANT+ devices.
  - Easier one-finger braking.
  - Lighter, stiffer caliper.
  - Larger pulleys; quieter shifting.
  - Sources: Gravel Cyclist, BikeRadar.
- **Weight:**
  - Claimed: 2,496 g, which most outlets say includes a PM (Cycling Weekly, Rouleur, Cyclist). BikeRadar gives 2,461 g without PM.
  - Claimed: about 150 g lighter than the previous Red; PM 29 g lighter (DC Rainmaker).
  - Measured by BikeRadar: 2,548 g (46/33 PM, 10-33) ([BikeRadar versus](https://www.bikeradar.com/features/tech/versus/shimano-dura-ace-di2-r9200-vs-sram-red-axs)).
  - Measured by BikeRadar: the 10-36 cassette weighed 226 g, against a 180 g claim that applies to the 10-28 ([BikeRadar hands-on](https://www.bikeradar.com/features/tech/2024-sram-red-axs-weights)).
- **Price (conflicting; depends on bundle):**
  - BikeRadar launch: from about £4,090 / $4,090 / €4,135 for a full groupset without PM.
  - BikeRadar review: €5,130 / £4,590 / $4,590.
  - Granfondo: €5,130 including PM crank and Karoo.
  - Cyclingnews: $4,200 with PM (including Karoo) and $3,700 without ([CN review](https://www.cyclingnews.com/reviews/sram-red-axs-review-excellent-braking-and-improved-shifting-in-a-lighter-overall-package/)).
  - Velo: $4,650.
  - Cycling Weekly: £4,248.
  - road.cc says the launch bundle included a Hammerhead Karoo (£449.99), and puts the crank at £700 without PM or £1,200 with PM.
  - **Conflict: check which bundle each outlet priced.**
- **Firmware:** no notable Red E1 firmware feature update found. Updates go through the AXS app.

### Red XPLR AXS (13-speed gravel), brief ⚠
- **Launch:** 2025 (exact date not confirmed). 1x13, 10-46 XG-1391 cassette (460% range).
- **Full Mount derailleur:** a direct-mount design based on T-Type MTB derailleurs. It needs a UDH frame ([BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/sram-aims-to-set-the-gravel-standard-with-red-axs-xplr)).
- **Weights:** cassette about 285–288 g (retailer claims).
- **Price (BikeRadar launch):**
  - With PM: £3,980 / $3,979 / €4,465.
  - Without PM: £3,530 / $3,529 / €3,960.
  - Cassette about US$600 (Escape Collective).
- **Verdicts:**
  - **Escape Collective:** praises Full Mount as the sturdier solution and likes the close steps. Criticises the limited range, lots of brake freestroke and the pricey fixed 10-46 cassette ([EC](https://escapecollective.com/sram-red-xplr-axs-e1-gravel-groupset-review/)).
  - **BikeRadar:** calls it the pinnacle of gravel groupsets so far, if your frame has UDH ([BikeRadar](https://bikeradar.com/reviews/components/groupsets/gravel-groupset/sram-red-xplr-axs-review)).

## 3. Campagnolo Super Record 13 (Super Record Wireless 13)

### Facts ⚠
- **Launch:** June 2025. Billed as the first 2x13 wireless road groupset ([road.cc](https://road.cc/content/tech-news/campagnolo-super-record-13-speed-thumb-shifter-returns-314241), [Cyclingnews](https://www.cyclingnews.com/news/thirteen-sprockets-and-thumb-shifters-campagnolo-launches-new-super-record-13-groupset/), [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/campagnolo-super-record-13)).
- **Wireless or wired:** wireless. Rechargeable, removable batteries on the front and rear derailleurs with magnetic charge ports (front about 34 g, rear about 38 g, retailer claims).
- **Battery claims ([Campagnolo](https://www.campagnolo.com/ww-de/super-record-13-akkus/CPOSUPERRECORD13.html)):** 750 km per charge, full charge in 60 min, over 20% after 10 min. The lever power source was not confirmed.
- **13-speed details:**
  - Road cassettes 10-29, 10-33, 11-32, 11-36. The 10-29 weighs 217 g (retailer claim).
  - 7 chainring options and 4 cassettes, which Campagnolo calls the widest choice on the market (Velo).
  - Road 2x chainring sizes not confirmed in the results. All-road 2x with Nano Clutch: 50/34, 48/32, 45/29.
  - 1x13 versions, plus the Super Record 13 X gravel version with a 9 t sprocket.
- **Features:**
  - Thumb shifter returns.
  - Titanium caliper bolts.
  - Campagnolo claims it is the fastest-shifting system.
  - Brake body 6 g lighter.
- **Weight:**
  - Claimed: 2,445 g without PM (Cyclist, Cycling Weekly, road.cc). BikeRadar lists 2,435 g, a conflict.
  - Claimed to be 3.7% lighter than SR Wireless 12 (2,520 g).
  - Measured by Velo: 2,560.6 g with a PM crank and a larger cassette ([Velo review](https://velo.outsideonline.com/road/road-gear/review-campagnolo-super-record-13-speed/)).
- **Price:**
  - Without PM: €4,300 / $4,750 / £3,900.
  - With PM: €5,399 / $5,899 / £4,950.
  - About €900 less than the 12-speed version (3bikes).
  - 1x13 road from €3,410 / $3,975 / £3,050.
  - Record 13 starts at €2,129.

## Review verdicts (paraphrased) ⚠

**Dura-Ace Di2 R9200**
- **BikeRadar (4.5/5):** arguably the best of the best, with class-leading shifting and quieter, stronger brakes. The gains cost a lot, and the paddles are hard to tell apart ([link](https://www.bikeradar.com/reviews/components/groupsets/groupset-road/shimano-dura-ace-di2-r9200-review)).
- **Velo (first ride):** steady refinement rather than a leap, with very fast upshifts, better brakes and more range ([link](https://velo.outsideonline.com/road/road-racing/shimano-dura-ace-di2-r9200-first-ride-review/)).
- **Rouleur:** smooth chainring changes under load; the 12-speed spread softens the jumps between gears ([link](https://rouleur.cc/blogs/desire-journal/shimano-dura-ace-9200-review)).

**SRAM Red AXS E1**
- **Cyclingnews:** excellent braking and better shifting in a lighter overall package (from the headline) ([link](https://www.cyclingnews.com/reviews/sram-red-axs-review-excellent-braking-and-improved-shifting-in-a-lighter-overall-package/)).
- **BikeRadar:** excellent braking and smoother, quieter shifting ([link](https://www.bikeradar.com/reviews/components/groupsets/groupset-road/sram-red-axs-review)). BikeRadar's versus piece calls it "the new standard to beat."
- **Cyclist (AU):** front shifting and braking are where it improves most ([link](https://cyclist.com.au/review-sram-red-axs/)).

**Campagnolo Super Record 13**
- **Velo:** "Campy's back on top." It fixes the complaints about the previous version and costs less. Minus points: the hood shape and no satellite shifter option ([link](https://velo.outsideonline.com/road/road-gear/review-campagnolo-super-record-13-speed/)).
- **Cycling Weekly:** "(almost) a knock out" ([link](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/reviews/gears-drivetrain/dont-call-it-a-come-back-campagnolos-new-13-speed-groupset-is-almost-a-knock-out)).
- **Cyclist (AU):** strong performance with a few quirks. Excellent brakes and fast rear shifts; front shifting is a notch below Dura-Ace ([link](https://cyclist.com.au/review-campagnolo-super-record-13/)).
- **Cyclist UK comparison:** gives the shifting category to Campagnolo ([link](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/news/campagnolo-super-record-13-vs-shimano-dura-ace-vs-sram-red-axs)).
