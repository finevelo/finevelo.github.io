## Summary of corrections (all ⚠ search-only, not verified on the page)
1. **Creo 2 groupset:** "Red XPLR" → **SRAM RED / XX1 Eagle AXS** (mullet). XPLR is only the Reverb dropper.
2. **Creo 2 160 Wh extender included:** **unconfirmed.** The "included with S-Works" evidence is for the gen-1 Creo SL.
3. **Creo 2 US price:** $14,399.99 list is confirmed; specialized.com also shows a **$13,499.99 sale price**. EU current price is €12,999 (retailer). UK current price not found.
4. **Creo 2 Expert** switched to **Shimano GRX Di2** (2026). The range continues as MY2027. No Creo 3.
5. **Cycling Weekly:** Turbo Creo 2 is the best overall pick in its 2026 e-gravel guide (tested as Expert Carbon, 4.5/5). The heading wording is unverified.
6. **Scott Addict Gravel Premium GBP:** £5,799 (BikeRadar) vs **£7,999** (road.cc and Cyclist). Use £7,999 pending Scott UK.
7. **BMC Kaius 01:** 1,469 g is the **frameset**; the **frame is 998 g** (54). Measured weights conflict: 7.97 kg (BikeRadar, unverified) vs 7.62 kg (Brujula).
8. **Factor Elara:** the Red XPLR + Black Inc 64 build is **$11,999 / €13,599 / £10,999**, not "from $10,099". Frameset £5,899. Official URL is /bikes/elara.
9. **Pinarello Grevil F9:** GBP £9,500 → **£10,000**. Frame 1,030 g (530, T900) is supported; 1,090 g has no source. Complete ~8.01 kg.
10. **Cervélo Áspero-5 Red:** the drivetrain is Red AXS + **XX SL mullet** (BikeRadar names the build "RED XPLR"). Reserve 48/53 GR, 8.27 kg, 1,023 g frame and 393 g fork are all unconfirmed.
11. **Pinarello Nytro:** the 11.4 kg is the **Road** E9. Do not use it for the Nytro Gravel rival (~13.3 kg).
12. **Trek "Checkpoint+ SLR"** does not exist. The range tops out at **Checkpoint+ SL 7 AXS**: $7,999, TQ HPR60, ~14.1 kg.
13. **New S-Works Turbo Levo SL 2 Di2** build at $17,999.99 (retailer), claimed 18.0 kg (S4). Check it if the Levo SL 2 row stays in.
