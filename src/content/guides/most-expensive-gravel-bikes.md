The most expensive gravel bike in our database is the **3T Racemax² Italia at $14,599** with Campagnolo Super Record 13. The **Specialized S-Works Crux 5 ($13,999.99)** and the **BMC Kaius 01 ONE ($12,999)** follow. Nearly every top build now uses SRAM Red XPLR AXS. The 3T stands apart with a two-by Campagnolo drivetrain.

Two points to keep in mind:

- **The ranking uses US list prices.** Some bikes rank very differently in Europe. Factor's Elara costs $11,399 in the US but €13,599 in Europe. The 3T Racemax² Italia is $14,599 in the US but €10,736 in Europe. Bikes without a confirmed US price (Scott Addict Gravel Premium, Look G85 Cezal RS) are left out of the ranking, whatever their European price.
- **Price and weight are unrelated at this level.** The lightest claims here are 7.1 kg for the BMC Kaius (size 54, without pedals) and the S-Works Crux 5 (size 56). BikeRadar weighed the Kaius at 7.97 kg. The Cervélo Áspero-5 costs $12,650 and is claimed at 8.27 kg.

The S-Works Crux 5 is covered in detail in our [launch analysis](/news/specialized-s-works-crux-5/).
