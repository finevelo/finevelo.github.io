The most expensive road bike in our database is the **Pinarello Dogma F at $15,750** (US retail, Dura-Ace Di2). Ridley's Falcn RS 2.0 ($14,999), Colnago's V5Rs ($14,750 with Dura-Ace Di2 and Enve SES 4.5 wheels) and Cervélo's R5 ($14,400) follow. Specialized's new S-Works Tarmac SL9 costs $13,999.99 with SRAM Red AXS ($13,499.99 with Dura-Ace Di2), effectively the same as the $14,000 the SL8 it replaced cost at launch.

US prices only. The Colnago Y1Rs has no confirmed US list price, so it is missing from this list even though its top build costs €16,500 in Europe.
