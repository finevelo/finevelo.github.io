The lightest complete road bike in our database is the **Scott Addict RC Ultimate at a claimed 5.9 kg** with SRAM Red AXS, which costs €12,999. Cervélo's R5 (5.97 kg) and Ridley's Falcn RS 2.0 (6.2 kg) follow. Every bike in the top three weighs less than the UCI's 6.8 kg minimum, so pro teams have to add ballast.

**Read the numbers with care.** These are manufacturer claims. Brands weigh different sizes, some leave out paint, and most quote the bike without pedals or bottle cages:

- **Scott** quotes its frames unpainted. Escape Collective puts the paint at roughly 40–50 g on the Ultimate.
- **Specialized** quotes 6.6 kg for a size 56 S-Works Tarmac SL9. TOUR weighed one at 6.49 kg, under the claim.
- **Trek** quotes 7.0 kg for a size ML Madone SLR 9 AXS. Escape Collective weighed a size M Project One sample at 7.13 kg with sealant, bottle cages and a computer mount.
- **Cannondale** quotes 6.35 kg for the SuperSix Evo LAB71 SL without naming a size.

Bikes without a published complete weight are left out, even when their frames are among the lightest; that includes the Colnago V5Rs (685 g frame, unpainted, size 48.5) and the Factor Ostro VAM, which Factor only describes as under 6.8 kg in a size 54.
