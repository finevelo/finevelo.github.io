The lightest complete gravel bikes in our database are the **BMC Kaius 01 ONE and the Specialized S-Works Crux 5, both claimed at 7.1 kg**, followed by Colnago's G5-X at 7.2 kg (size 48). All three use SRAM Red XPLR AXS.

These are manufacturer claims, and they do not use the same size or tyres. BikeRadar weighed the Kaius at 7.97 kg, almost 0.9 kg over the claim. Bikes without a published complete weight (Factor Elara, Pinarello Grevil F9, 3T Racemax² Italia) are left out of the ranking.
