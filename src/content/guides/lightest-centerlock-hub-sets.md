The lightest Centerlock hub set in our database is the **NonPlus Primaro Ultra SP at a claimed 187 g**. The DT Swiss 240 EXP, which many premium wheelsets are built on, weighs a claimed 296 g. That makes the lightest set 109 g lighter than the industry benchmark.

All hubs here share the same standard:

- Centerlock disc mount
- 12 × 100 mm front and 12 × 142 mm rear thru-axles
- Road or gravel use

Most weights refer to the SRAM XDR freehub with 24 spoke holes. The freehub body alone can change the weight by several grams: DT Swiss's 180 EXP is 7 g heavier with a Shimano HG body. Each entry notes the version the weight refers to.

**These are manufacturer figures.** None of them has yet been confirmed on an independent scale we could check. Treat a gap of a few grams as a tie, and look at bearings, serviceability and spoke type as well as the number.

Two things stand out:

- **The top of the list is German and Italian.** Small makers in Baden-Württemberg (NonPlus, Tune) and Italy (Extralite, Carbon-Ti) all come in below DT Swiss's top hub.
- **Light does not have to mean straight-pull.** If your wheelbuilder prefers J-bend spokes, Tune's Micro CL is the lightest option we found at 349 g.
