The most expensive e-bike in our database is the **Specialized S-Works Turbo Creo 2 at $14,399.99** (US list price in 2026), a 12.87 kg (claimed, size 56) e-road and gravel bike with a 320 Wh battery. Lightweight e-bikes from Specialized, Pinarello and Cervélo dominate the top of the list. Speed pedelecs such as the Stromer ST7 (from €11,490 in Europe) are left out, because no confirmed US price exists.

Prices are manufacturer list prices; weights are manufacturer claims unless a row notes otherwise. Our [Creo 2 analysis](/news/specialized-turbo-creo-2-s-works/) covers how the S-Works compares with its rivals.
