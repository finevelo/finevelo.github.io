Parlee's Z-Zero GT is the company's flagship road bike: a hand-built, tube-to-tube carbon frame that Parlee sells as a frameset for $12,490 (including bar, stem and seatpost) and as a complete bike from $16,490 to $22,990. Parlee claims a frame weight of 893g for a 53cm size.

## What is the Z-Zero GT?

It is the current generation of Parlee's Z-Series, a road platform the company says it has refined for more than 25 years and still builds by hand in a small shop 45 minutes north of Boston. Parlee pitches the GT at long, fast days in the mountains, giving comfort the same billing as speed, and says it will handle tarmac, farm roads and "the occasional gravel".

## What changed from the previous Z-Zero?

According to Parlee:

- A revised layup and design cut 187g compared with the predecessor.
- An all-carbon, UDH-compatible chainstay module is described as stronger, lighter and stiffer.
- The fully integrated fork is designed and built in-house, routes all cables internally, accepts one-piece bar-stems and clears 40mm tyres.
- The bottom bracket sits 10mm lower than on "typical road bikes", by Parlee's comparison. Published BB drop is 80mm on most sizes and 77mm on the two largest.

Parlee also calls the GT the fastest Z-Series it has made and cites improved aerodynamics, but publishes no aero data.

## How does the custom side work?

There are twelve standard sizes: six in Race geometry and six in Sport. Even a stock-sized GT ships with rider-specific crank length, stem length, bar width, seatpost setback and saddle width. Parlee lists the tubing as Z-Zero specific, with rider- and size-specific stiffness options.

Custom geometry is a $990 option. Parlee says a fully bespoke GT also tailors the frame composition to the rider. Paint comes in 20 standard colours or a nude waxed carbon finish, with Parlee Paint Lab custom work available. A further $990 extended paint option colour-matches the cockpit, headset, cages and post.

How the two stock geometries differ, size M:

| Size M | Race | Sport |
|---|---|---|
| Stack | 552mm | 572mm |
| Reach | 388mm | 372mm |
| Head angle | 72° | 71.5° |
| Chainstay | 422mm | 427mm |
| Wheelbase | 1005mm | 1006mm |
| Trail | 63mm | 66mm |

## Who is it for?

On Parlee's own description, the GT suits a rider who wants a fitted, hand-made road bike for long and hilly days rather than an off-the-peg race frame. The threaded T47 bottom bracket, UDH dropout and 40mm clearance point towards practicality and longevity. The $990 custom-geometry option is small relative to the frameset price.

We have not ridden the GT, so claims about ride quality remain Parlee's.
