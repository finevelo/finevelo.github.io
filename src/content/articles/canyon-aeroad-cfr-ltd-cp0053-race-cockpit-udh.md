Canyon updated its Aeroad CFR and Aeroad CFR LTD on 25 June 2026, timed for the Tour de France. Canyon's release does not list prices or complete-bike weights, so both are TBC here. What it does offer is a headline figure: Canyon says the LTD configuration recorded 198 W of drag under TOUR Magazine's wind tunnel protocol, and that its CP0053 RACE cockpit is 120 g lighter than the bar it replaces.

## What actually changed?

The Aeroad CFR platform was released in 2024 and is raced by Alpecin-Premier Tech, CANYON//SRAM and Movistar. Canyon describes this as an upgrade to the frame and specification, built around four changes:

- The CP0053 RACE Bar, a one-piece V-shaped cockpit Canyon describes as previously pro-only
- A UDH interface at the drive-side dropout
- More seatpost and wheelset choices through MyCanyon
- Revised drop options

## How does the RACE bar compare with the PACE bar?

| | CP0053 RACE vs CP0048 PACE (Canyon's claims) |
|---|---|
| Weight | 120 g lighter |
| Drag (wind tunnel) | 2 W lower |
| Drag (real world) | Up to 25 W lower |
| Flare in drops | 14° |
| Effective reach | +10 mm |
| Effective stack | −20 mm |
| Torsional stiffness | Higher |
| Widths | 350 and 375 mm |
| Lengths | Seven |
| Computer mounts | Two carbon mounts included |

The two drag numbers should be read separately. Canyon attributes 2 W to the bar itself. The "up to 25-watts" figure comes from reducing the rider's frontal area in the lower position the bar allows. That gain depends on whether you can hold that position.

## What does the 198 W figure represent?

Canyon says TOUR tests at the GST wind tunnel in Immenstaad, Germany, at a steady 45 km/h, using a dummy with moving legs and a yaw range of −20° to +20°. The tested bike had the following build:

- SRAM Red AXS 1x13 with a 10-46T cassette
- AXS E1 hydraulic brakes
- DT Swiss ARC 1100 Dicut db 65 mm wheels
- Continental Aero 111 26 mm and GP 5000 28 mm tyres
- CP0053 cockpit
- SP0105 seatpost
- Selle Italia SLR Boost 3D Carbonio saddle

It is a specific configuration; a different build will measure differently.

Both Scope wheelsets use the Dutch brand's textured, scale-like rim surface.

## What are the drop options?

Classic drops are standard. Race drops succeed Canyon's earlier Aero drops, with slightly less flare and more drop for sprints and descents. Compact drops shorten effective reach by 10 mm compared with Classic, for riders who prioritise ergonomics.

## Can existing owners fit the RACE bar?

Canyon says the CP0053 will be sold aftermarket and is backwards compatible with any bike using the CP0048 PACE Bar. That includes the latest Aeroad, Endurace and Ultimate in CFR and CF SLX form. Aftermarket pricing is TBC. Note that the RACE Bar does without the PACE Bar's gear groove and adaptive cockpit system.

## Who is it for?

The RACE Bar is aimed at riders who race, or ride as if they do, and can use a lower, longer front end: Canyon quotes 20 mm less effective stack and 10 mm more effective reach. The UDH change is a practical gain for anyone, making replacement hangers easier to source. Lower down the range, the Aeroad CF SLX now gets MyCanyon choices for crank length, stem length, drops and cassette.

## How much does it cost and where can you buy it?

Prices are TBC. Canyon says the Aeroad CFR and CFR LTD are available from 25 June 2026, only via Canyon.com and the Canyon App. There are five new colours: Prima Nero, Alba and Iride, Fabrio Cosmic Ash and Mano Orion.
