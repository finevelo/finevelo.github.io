The SuperX LAB71 is the top build of Cannondale's gravel race platform, the version the brand says stands "at the apex" of gravel racing performance. It pairs the Series 0 carbon frameset with SRAM RED XPLR AXS, a power meter crankset and Reserve carbon wheels on DT Swiss 180 hubs. Cannondale's US product page lists neither a price nor a complete-bike weight, so both are TBC here. The brand's only weight claim is that the LAB71 SuperX is more than 100 grams lighter than its predecessor.

## What is the SuperX LAB71?

It is a drop-bar race bike for fast, mass-start gravel events. Cannondale frames it around the speed of modern racing, noting that average speeds are "well over 35 km/h", and pitches the bike at elite gravel racing and KOM hunting. The frame and fork use Cannondale's Series 0 carbon construction.

## What does the frame do differently?

Cannondale says its Proportional Response construction is tuned by size, with different carbon types and moduli oriented so that each frame size gets its own balance of weight, stiffness and durability.

Comfort comes from designed flex zones in the seat tube, rear triangle and top tube. These work with a D-shaped carbon seatpost that Cannondale says adds both aero benefit and vertical compliance.

Up front, the OutFront geometry combines a slack head angle with a 55 mm fork offset, longer than usual. Cannondale says this adds stability on loose, steep ground without dulling quick line changes. A Delta steerer (1-1/8" to 1-1/2") allows cable routing from the one-piece SystemBar R-One cockpit into the frame and reduces frontal area.

Tyre clearance is generous for a race frame: up to 48 mm in the frame and 51 mm in the fork, with at least 4 mm each side, according to Cannondale. A hidden top-tube bag mount sits under a cover.

The 40T chainring with a 10-46 cassette gives a lowest gear slightly under 1:1. That is a race-oriented spread rather than a loaded-touring one. Extras include Reserve Fillmore valves, an AXS charger and a Garmin/Wahoo computer mount.

## How does sizing work?

Cannondale's spec lists sizes from 46 to 61 cm, and parts scale with them. Cranks run from 165 mm (46 cm) to 175 mm (61 cm). The integrated bar/stem runs from 90x400 mm on the 46 and 51 cm sizes to 120x420 mm on the 61 cm. The seatpost has 0 mm offset on the 46 cm and 15 mm on the rest. Because the cockpit is one piece, fit changes mean swapping the whole unit, so it is worth settling fit before purchase.

## Who is it for?

Cannondale is clear that this is a race bike for riders chasing podiums and records. The integrated power meter and aero cockpit point the same way. It is not single-purpose, though: Cannondale says that with 33c tyres it works as a cyclocross bike, helped by mud clearance and a flat top tube for shouldering.

The frame carries Cannondale's limited lifetime warranty. Cannondale components get one year, and some parts have additional coverage from their makers.
