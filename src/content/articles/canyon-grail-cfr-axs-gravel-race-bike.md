## What is the Grail CFR AXS?

The Grail CFR AXS is the top SRAM build of Canyon's gravel race platform. It costs $8,999 in the US (from €8,000 in Germany), and Canyon claims 17.6 lb (7.98 kg) for a size M. The build pairs SRAM Red XPLR with a power meter and 50 mm DT Swiss GRC 1100 carbon wheels.

Canyon pitches it plainly as a race bike. Canyon says the CFR layup is the lightest in the Grail range, and the frame is stripped back, with no fender mounts.

## How does it sit in the Grail range?

| Platform | US price from | Claimed frame weight |
|---|---|---|
| Grail CFR | $7,999 | 990 g |
| Grail CF SLX | $4,999 | 1,090 g |
| Grail CF SL | $3,499 | 1,130 g |

There is one discrepancy. The range page gives 990 g for the CFR frame, but the spec sheet for this build lists 1,104 g for a size M frame and 449 g for the fork. Canyon does not explain the gap. Canyon also offers the CFR through its MyCanyon custom programme.

## What's notable about the frame?

**Tyre clearance.** According to Canyon, both ends accept 57 mm (2.25 in) tyres, leaving 4 mm of ISO clearance. To make room, the bike is 1x only, and the drive-side chainstay is solid carbon.

**Aerodynamics.** Canyon says it optimised the Grail for 35 km/h, which it calls real-world race pace. It also says the bike came out ahead in wind-tunnel benchmarking against rivals including Cervélo's Aspero-5. We have not seen the data, so treat this as a manufacturer claim.

**Fit.** Canyon says the Grail now has 15 mm less stack than before, for a lower race position. The PACE Bar cockpit adjusts by 50 mm in width and 20 mm in height using a single TX25 tool. Canyon says this needs no steerer cut and no brake bleed.

**Comfort and storage.** Canyon says the SP0093 VCLS Aero seatpost offers over 25% more vertical compliance than a rigid equivalent. The LOAD down-tube storage has room for a pump, tools, a tube and CO2, though these items are sold separately.

Seven sizes run from 2XS to 2XL. The two smallest sizes use slacker head angles of 68.5° and 70°. Chainstays stay at 435 mm across the range. Canyon sets a maximum system weight of 120 kg for rider, luggage and bike.

## Who is it for?

This bike is for riders who race gravel and want aero gains, data and room for big tyres in one package. Canyon notes that Rosa Klöser won the 2026 Traka 360 on the Grail.

Riders who want fender mounts or a cheaper route into the Grail platform should look at the CF SLX. If you prefer Shimano, the Grail CFR Di2 uses the CFR frame with Shimano GRX RX827 Di2 and DT Swiss GRC 1100 wheels for $7,999.
