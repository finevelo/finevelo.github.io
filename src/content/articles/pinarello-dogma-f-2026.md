The Pinarello Dogma F you can buy today is the frame Pinarello launched on 20 June 2024. At launch, complete bikes started at **$14,500, €14,500 or £12,600**. Pinarello no longer shows prices on its own site. Two years on, a US retailer lists the 2026 **Dura-Ace Di2 build at $15,750**. European retailers ask between €14,500 and €16,400; the top figure is for the Netcompany INEOS replica on Scope wheels.

For that Dura-Ace Di2 bike, Pinarello claims **6.77 kg** in size 53 without pedals. That is a good number for an aero race bike, but it no longer puts the Dogma at the front of the class on the scales.

## How much does the Dogma F cost?

Complete bikes come in three builds: Shimano Dura-Ace Di2, SRAM Red AXS and Campagnolo Super Record Wireless. The frameset launched at **$6,950 / €6,700 / £5,500**.

Prices have moved since launch. Reviews in late 2024 were already quoting £12,500 to £13,000 in the UK for the Dura-Ace build. The $15,750 US figure is for the 2026 Dura-Ace Di2 bike on Princeton Peak 4550 wheels, and it has no power meter. Reviewers have criticised that before: at this price, a power meter should come as standard.

| Build (size 53, claimed) | Wheels | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| SRAM Red AXS | Princeton Peak 4550 | 6.63 kg |
| Shimano Dura-Ace Di2 | Princeton Peak 4550 | 6.77 kg |
| Campagnolo Super Record 13 Wireless | Campagnolo Bora WTO 45 | 6.88 kg |

Independent scales broadly agree. road.cc weighed a size 55 Dura-Ace Di2 bike at 6.82 kg. BikeRadar weighed Tom Pidcock's own 2026 Dogma F at the Tour at 6.8 kg without pedals; that bike is a size 53 with SRAM Red and Zipp 454 NSW wheels.

At launch, BikeRadar reported 860 g for an unpainted size 53 frame. Pinarello's own figure covers the whole package: 1,925 g for frame, fork, seatpost, axles, headset and bar (road.cc).

The 2024 update refined the frame rather than redesigning it. Pinarello claims the frameset is **108 g lighter** than the 2021 Dogma F:

- Just under half of the saving comes from the new MOST Talon Ultra Fast cockpit, which is about 40 g lighter on its own.
- The rest comes from slimmer head tube and bottom bracket areas, and from higher-modulus carbon around the bottom bracket and chainstays.

The frame is now made from Toray M40X. The other changes:

- **Narrower front end.** An oval steerer made an 8 mm narrower head tube possible. The catch is that the Talon Ultra Fast is the only cockpit that fits. Its drops flare by 1 cm per side, so a 40 cm bar measures 38 cm at the hoods.
- **Aero-Keel.** Where the down tube meets the bottom bracket, it is rotated by 3.5°. Overall, Pinarello claims only a 0.2% improvement in drag area. Cyclingnews put that at less than a watt at 40 km/h. Pinarello and the team describe it as worth about 175 g over a Grand Tour.
- **Tyre clearance** rose from 28 mm to an official **30 mm**.
- **Fork rake** grew from 43 mm to 47 mm. The rest of the geometry is largely unchanged.

| | Dogma F (2024–) | Dogma F (2021–24) |
|---|---|---|
| Launched | 20 June 2024 | 24 June 2021 |
| Frame weight (claimed) | 860 g (53, unpainted; frameset 108 g lighter) | 865 g (53, unpainted) |
| Tyre clearance | 30 mm | 28 mm |
| Carbon | Toray M40X | Toray T1100 or T1000 (sources differ) |
| Frameset price at launch | $6,950 | $6,950 |

## How does it compare with its rivals?

This is where two years of age show. Cervélo launched its R5 in September 2025 and claims 5.97 kg for the complete bike. Specialized replaced the Tarmac SL8 with the SL9 in June 2026: it costs less, weighs less and clears 32 mm tyres. Our [SL9 vs Dogma F comparison](/news/s-works-tarmac-sl9-vs-dogma-f/) goes into detail.

| | Pinarello Dogma F | S-Works Tarmac SL9 | Cervélo R5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top-build price | $15,750 (2026 retail) | $13,499.99 (list, 2026) | $14,250 (launch, 2025) |
| Groupset | Dura-Ace Di2 | Dura-Ace Di2 | SRAM Red AXS |
| Frame weight (claimed) | 860 g (53, unpainted) | 687 g (56) | 651 g (56) |
| Complete weight | 6.77 kg claimed (53) | 6.6 kg claimed (56) | 5.97 kg claimed |

The Dogma was never built to top the weight table, and reviewers praise it for other things:

- **Cyclingnews** scored it 94% and called it probably the most complete road bike its tester had ridden. Value was the main reason for lost marks.
- **BikeRadar** singled out its descending and its balanced, quiet ride, but noted the limited tyre clearance.
- **road.cc** praised the handling at every speed and the low weight for an aero bike. Its tester found that the painted seatpost scratches easily.

## Who rides it?

Pinarello has supplied the Ineos team since its Team Sky days. Since the 2026 Giro the team has raced as **Netcompany INEOS**, now on Scope wheels with Dura-Ace Di2.

For 2026 Pinarello also became title sponsor of **Pinarello–Q36.5**, which put Tom Pidcock on a Dogma F. His season includes wins at Milano–Torino and the Andorra MoraBanc Clàssica, and second place at Milan–San Remo.

## Is a new Dogma F coming?

Nothing credible suggests so:

- The 2027 model year adds four new finishes on the unchanged frame: Molten Sand, Obsidian Emerald, Phantom Ice and Cobalt Phantom.
- Pinarello's newest launch is the Dogma X endurance bike, released in July 2026 from £12,500.
- Talk of a new Dogma F in late 2027 or 2028 is speculation, with no spy shots or UCI listing to back it up.

## The verdict

Cervélo and Specialized both beat the Dogma F on weight and on price. What you buy it for is the handling, which reviewers consistently rank among the best. You also buy a bike line that the Ineos team has raced since its Team Sky days.

If you can live with 30 mm tyres and a cockpit that only Pinarello makes, the Dogma F remains the reference point. Only wait for a new generation if you are willing to wait at least another year.
