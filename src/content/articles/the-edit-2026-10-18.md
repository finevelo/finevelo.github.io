Gravel race bikes are being rebuilt around aerodynamics and bigger tyres. Climbing bikes keep pushing below the UCI's 6.8 kg limit. Prices at the top keep rising. Everything here is the manufacturer's claim as reported at launch; where a figure is missing, it has not been announced.

## 1. Ridley's Falcn RS 2.0 drops to 6.2 kg

Ridley's climbing bike is now **$14,999 / €12,499 / £11,299** with SRAM Red AXS. The new frame is claimed at **705 g** in size M, down from 825 g. Frame, fork and seatpost together are 12% lighter.

The Red build weighs a claimed **6.2 kg** (M). Ridley says every build comes in under the UCI's 6.8 kg minimum, so pro teams will need ballast.

| Build | USD | EUR | GBP |
|---|---|---|---|
| SRAM Red AXS | $14,999 | €12,499 | £11,299 |
| SRAM Force AXS | $10,199 | €8,499 | £7,699 |
| Shimano Ultegra Di2 | $8,999 | €7,499 | £6,899 |

**Why it matters:** even the cheapest build sits above our $8,000 road threshold. The Belgian brand is pricing its Red build like a Pinarello or Specialized flagship in the US, but in Europe it is €2,000 cheaper than the [Dogma F](/news/pinarello-dogma-f-2026/). On sale from 8 October. *Source: [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/2027-ridley-falcn-rs-2)*

## 2. Colnago's G5-X takes aim at the Crux

Colnago's new race gravel bike has a claimed **950 g frame** (M), down from 1,050–1,120 g, and a 420 g fork. It clears **53 mm tyres**.

The top build costs **€9,200 / £9,999** and comes with SRAM Red XPLR AXS and Zipp 303 XPLR SW wheels. It weighs a claimed **7.2 kg** (size 48, with 50 mm tyres). There is also a Rival build at €4,900 / £5,299. No US price or release date yet.

**Why it matters:** it is a direct answer to the [S-Works Crux 5](/news/specialized-s-works-crux-5/), and €4,800 cheaper in Europe. The Crux frame is 161 g lighter and clears 2 mm more tyre. *Source: [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/2027-colnago-g5-x)*

## 3. Moots brings back the titanium all-road bike

The new Moots County Line is a titanium all-road bike from the Colorado builder:

- **Frameset:** $5,499
- **Complete bikes:** $9,999 (Force 1x), $10,599 (Force 2x), $12,499 (Red 1x) and $12,999 (Red 2x)

It is available now. A lighter RSL version with double-butted tubing follows in early 2027; no price yet. Moots has not published weights.

**Why it matters:** custom-feeling titanium at superbike prices. It shows that demand for handmade metal at the top of the market is holding up. *Source: [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/2027-moots-county-line)*

## 4. Cadex's first XC wheels: carbon spokes, 1,248 g, $4,000

Cadex has launched its first mountain bike wheelset, the Max XC, at **$4,000 / €4,200 / £3,500**. In the US, wheels are also sold singly: $1,650 front, $2,350 rear.

- **Weight:** a claimed **1,248 g** with tape and valves (1,208 g without)
- **Rims:** 29 inch, 22 mm deep, 30 mm internal width, hookless
- **Spokes:** carbon, bonded into the hub flanges
- **Freehubs:** SRAM XD or Shimano Micro Spline
- **Warranty:** lifetime

**Why it matters:** this is now the price ceiling for XC wheels. Giant fits them to its new Anthem SL Special Edition, Cadex's sister brand. *Source: [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/cadex-max-xc-wheelsystem)*

## 5. Fire at Atherton Bikes' factory in Wales

A fire broke out at Atherton Bikes' factory in Machynlleth on Sunday 11 October. Around 30 firefighters attended and nobody was injured.

The extent of the damage is still being assessed. Atherton expects disruption to production and deliveries and has not confirmed new dispatch dates.

**Why it matters:** Atherton builds premium mountain bikes from 3D-printed titanium lugs and carbon tubes. If you have one on order, expect a delay. *Source: [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/firefighters-tackle-heartbreaking-blaze-at-atherton-factory-in-wales)*

## Also noted

The Force XPLR build is €6,990 and weighs 8.3 kg; the Red build weighs 7.9 kg, price not yet announced. *[BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/2027-look-g85-cezal-rs)*
- **Shimano Dura-Ace 13-speed:** prototypes have been raced at the Vuelta and the World Championships. Shimano has not confirmed a name, specs or a launch date, so treat it as a rumour for now. *[Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/mathieu-van-der-poel-rides-13-speed-dura-ace-prototype-at-uci-world-championships)*
