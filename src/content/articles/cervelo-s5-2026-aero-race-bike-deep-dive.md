The Cervélo S5 is the brand's dedicated aero race bike, and the current generation sits firmly in high-end territory: five complete builds run from $10,100 to $14,500. Cervélo does not publish a complete-bike weight, so that figure is TBC. What it does claim is that this S5 is 6.3 watts faster and 124 grams lighter than its predecessor.

## What is the S5, and why does it matter?

Cervélo positions the S5 as a benchmark for speed and aerodynamic efficiency. The brand points to its record at the front of the professional peloton. It cites Wout van Aert's Green Jersey, won by what Cervélo calls the biggest margin in modern history, and Simon Yates taking the maglia rosa. The recognisable V-stem and bayonet fork remain part of the design.

## Where do the aero gains come from?

Cervélo says most of the 6.3-watt saving comes from the front end, where fork and head tube depth have both increased. A second source is the pairing of an asymmetric seat tube with an asymmetric rear wheel. The two shapes are matched so that air flows smoothly over both.

That rear wheel is part of the Reserve 57|64 wheelset, which Reserve and Cervélo co-developed for this frame. Cervélo claims it is 3 watts faster than the Reserve 52|63 at the same weight.

## How did it get lighter with more surface area?

Deeper tubes usually add grams. Cervélo acknowledges that the larger fork and head tube did, but says a revised layup and lighter hardware made the system lighter overall. The HB19 one-piece bar and stem is credited with a significant share of the claimed 124-gram reduction.

## How does the one-piece cockpit handle fit?

Integrated cockpits trade adjustability for weight and drag. Cervélo addresses this in three ways:

- **Plus-Four shape:** the bar is 4cm narrower at the hoods than in the drops (for example 38/42cm). The aim is a more aero position on the hoods and stability in the drops. All sizes are UCI-legal, Cervélo says.
- **15 size combinations**, with sizing help from an authorised retailer at no extra cost.
- **A one-time swap:** if you are the first owner of a new bike, Cervélo will change the bar/stem to a different size once without charge, provided you ask within 60 days. Labour and installation parts may be extra.

Trail is held at 55.5 to 55.6mm across the range, while head angle and fork offset change by size. Cervélo says the new S5 retains the handling, poise and stability of its predecessor.

## Who is it for?

Cervélo pitches the S5 as a race bike. It says the weight saving makes it better "on the flats and on the climbs alike". The electronic-only requirement and one-piece cockpit make it less suited to anyone who wants mechanical shifting or frequent position changes. For riders planning wider tyres, the figure to note is the 34mm measured clearance.

Between builds, the step from the Force/Ultegra tier to the Red/Dura-Ace tier is $4,100 to $4,400. The frame and HB19 cockpit are common to all five.
