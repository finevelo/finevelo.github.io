BMC's Teammachine SLR 01 tops out at **USD 14,599** list for the SLR 01 ONE, shown on BMC's US store at a sale price of **USD 11,699**. The range starts at USD 6,199. Claimed weight: **TBC**, as BMC's listing does not state one.

BMC presents its race bikes as the product of long racing experience and its latest technology. Below, we stick to what the company publishes: builds, prices and how the SLR 01 sits next to its sibling.

## What is the Teammachine SLR 01?

It is one of two families under BMC's road racing heading, alongside the Teammachine R series. On price, the 01 is the higher tier within the SLR family. Below it sit Teammachine SLR ONE, TWO and THREE builds listed from USD 2,899 to USD 4,799.

BMC's collection page does not break out frame details, groupsets or wheels per build. Those remain **TBC** here.

## Is there a frameset-only option?

Not for the SLR 01, based on BMC's US road racing listing. Riders who want to build from a bare frame are pointed, at least on this page, towards the R 01.

## Who is it for?

BMC lists the Teammachine line under road racing, and the SLR 01 carries the highest prices in the SLR family. Its builds span from a USD 14,599 flagship down to USD 6,199, so the name covers a wide range of budgets.

For buyers, the practical point is timing. With the ONE and TWO discounted by USD 2,900 and USD 3,000 respectively, the gap between list and sale is large enough to matter. BMC's collection page does not say what you get for that money in groupset, wheels and weight, so check the individual build pages before committing.

We have not ridden the Teammachine SLR 01, and nothing here is a verdict on how it rides.
