Princeton CarbonWorks lists the Wake 6560 Evolution II at 3,150 to 4,500 on its own web store (the product page shows no currency symbol). The lightest build, on Tactic Racing TR01 v2 hubs, has a claimed weight of 1,250g. Princeton calls it the sixth iteration of its flagship wheel, and it is worth looking at what has changed and what has stayed the same.

## What is the Wake 6560?

According to Princeton, the Wake 6560 was conceived in 2012, with the first rideable versions finished in 2015. The spec sheet gives the rim depth as varying between 60mm and 65mm. The brand positions it as an all-rounder: it claims the wheel is aerodynamically faster than deeper designs while handling better and weighing less than shallower ones.

The Evolution II is described as Princeton's most aerodynamically efficient "mass-start compliant" road wheel, which places it as a road racing wheel rather than a time-trial specialist.

## What's new vs. the Evolution I?

The headline change is weight. Princeton says an all-new carbon layup brings the rim from 470g to 420g, while still exceeding 120J in impact testing. Across the wheelset, the brand quotes a 10% mass reduction compared with the Evolution I.

The rim profile is built around a 28mm tyre, using a 22mm internal width and a maximum external width of 33mm. The bead is fully hooked.

Princeton also says the Evolution II rims arrive pre-balanced, intended to reduce vibration and harmonics at speed.

## What hasn't changed?

Princeton has kept steel spokes, citing safety, reliability and ease of maintenance. Builds use 24 front and 24 rear Hyperlite Aero spokes. The tyre bed remains non-drilled, so there is no tubeless tape to fit or replace. Tubeless valves and padded wheel bags are included.

## What colours are available?

There are ten finishes in total. The six carried over from the standard range cover black and white, each offered in either a matte or a gloss surface, plus gold and chrome, which come only in gloss. The Evolution II family, beginning with this wheel, adds four further gloss options: sand, cobalt, red and chameleon.

## Who is it for?

On paper, the Wake 6560 Evolution II suits a rider who wants one deep wheelset for road racing and fast group riding, runs 28mm tyres, and values hooked rims and conventional steel spokes over the last few grams. The claimed 1,250g applies only to the Tactic TR01 v2 build. The other hub options add between 70g and 205g.

The choice of hub, freehub and finish gives a high degree of personalisation without a custom order. For rim-brake versions or fully custom builds, Princeton asks riders to contact it directly. We have not ridden the Evolution II, so performance claims remain the manufacturer's.
