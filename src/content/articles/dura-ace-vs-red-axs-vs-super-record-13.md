The three flagship road groupsets now weigh within about 100 g of each other, at **2.4–2.6 kg** for a complete disc-brake groupset. Without power meter, their US list prices differ by about $660, and they are at very different stages of their lives:

- **Campagnolo Super Record 13** (June 2025) is the newest. It costs **€4,300 / $4,750 / £3,900** without power meter and is claimed at **2,445 g**.
- **SRAM Red AXS** (May 2024) is claimed at **2,461 g** without and **2,496 g** with power meter. US launch prices ran from about **$4,090** without power meter.
- **Shimano Dura-Ace Di2 R9200** dates from **August 2021**. Shimano claimed **2,438.5 g** without power meter; BikeRadar weighed it at **2,514 g** with power meter. It launched at just under **£3,700** without power meter.

## How do the three compare?

| | Shimano Dura-Ace Di2 R9200 | SRAM Red AXS (E1) | Campagnolo Super Record 13 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launched | 31 August 2021 | May 2024 | June 2025 |
| Speeds | 2×12 | 2×12 (1× available) | 2×13 (1× available) |
| Cassettes | 11-28, 11-30, 11-34 | 10-28, 10-30, 10-33, 10-36 | 10-29, 10-33, 11-32, 11-36 |
| Wireless | Wireless levers; derailleurs wired to one battery | Fully wireless, battery on each derailleur | Wireless, rechargeable battery on each derailleur |
| Claimed weight | 2,438.5 g (without power meter) | 2,461 g without, 2,496 g with power meter | 2,445 g (without power meter) |
| Measured weight | 2,514 g and 2,579 g, both with power meter (BikeRadar) | 2,548 g with power meter (BikeRadar) | 2,561 g with power meter (Velo) |
| Price, no power meter | just under £3,700 (launch) | about $4,090 / €4,135 / £4,090 (launch) | €4,300 / $4,750 / £3,900 |
| Price, with power meter | TBC | $4,590 / €5,130 / £4,590 | €5,399 / $5,899 / £4,950 |

The measured weights are not like-for-like. Each outlet weighed different chainrings, cassettes and cranks. Campagnolo's claim also has two versions: most outlets quote 2,445 g, BikeRadar 2,435 g. SRAM prices vary between outlets because some launch bundles included a Hammerhead Karoo head unit.

## Super Record 13: is Campagnolo back?

Campagnolo calls Super Record 13 the first 2×13 wireless road groupset. Velo's review headline was simply "Campy's back on top". Key points:

- **Thirteen sprockets** with four road cassettes, starting at 10 or 11 teeth.
- **The thumb shifter is back,** a Campagnolo hallmark that the previous wireless group had dropped.
- **Battery:** Campagnolo claims 750 km per charge and a full charge in 60 minutes.
- **Price:** about €900 less than the 12-speed version it replaces, according to 3bikes.

Reviewers' criticisms: Cyclist found front shifting a notch behind Dura-Ace, and Velo missed a satellite-shifter option. Cycling Weekly called the groupset "(almost) a knock out".

## SRAM Red AXS: what did the 2024 update fix?

The E1 Red mainly addressed the lever and the brakes:

- **New hoods and levers,** 83 g lighter than before, with up to 15 mm reach adjustment.
- **Easier one-finger braking** and a lighter caliper.
- **Larger pulley wheels** for smoother shifting on the smallest sprockets.

Cyclingnews summed it up as excellent braking and improved shifting in a lighter package. BikeRadar's comparison called it the new standard to beat.

## Dura-Ace R9200: when does Shimano answer?

Dura-Ace is five years old, and reviewers still rate its shifting highly, although Cyclist found Super Record 13's rear shifting may now outmatch the Shimano benchmark. BikeRadar gave it 4.5 out of 5 and called it arguably the best of the best, though it criticised the shift paddles as hard to tell apart.

A successor is visible. Alpecin–Premier Tech raced a **13-speed Dura-Ace prototype** at the 2026 Vuelta, and Mathieu van der Poel used one at the world championships in Montréal. Shimano has not announced it. Cycling Weekly expects a release in early 2027 at the earliest. The first R9300-numbered parts are already on sale: new R9370 carbon-spoke wheels and PD-R9300 pedals.

**If you are buying a Dura-Ace bike now,** bear in mind that the 12-speed groupset is likely to be replaced within months. It will still work for years.

## Who uses what in the WorldTour?

For 2026, Cyclingnews counts **10 of the 18** men's WorldTeams on Shimano. SRAM supplies **7 or 8**, depending on whether you count by BikeRadar's or Velo's list; BikeRadar notes EF Education and Decathlon moving to SRAM. No men's WorldTeam races Campagnolo, according to BikeRadar.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Dura-Ace Di2** if you want the most refined shifting today and do not mind buying near the end of a product cycle.
- **SRAM Red AXS** if you want fully wireless installation, wide gearing and the easiest switch to 1×.
- **Super Record 13** if you want one of the lightest claimed weights, 13 sprockets and the thumb shifter, and you are happy to commit to Campagnolo-compatible wheels.
