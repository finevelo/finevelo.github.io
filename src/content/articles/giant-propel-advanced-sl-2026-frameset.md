Giant's Propel Advanced SL is the brand's aero road race frame in its highest composite grade. In its 2026 form, Giant sells it in the US as a frameset for $4,300 MSRP. Giant does not list a weight, so that figure is TBC, but the company claims the frameset is 225 grams lighter than the previous generation and offers "an even greater stiffness-to-weight advantage".

## What is the Propel Advanced SL meant to do?

Giant frames it around the decisive moments of a race: the final 200 metres of a sprint, closing a gap, a solo attack. Its summary is "more aero, more adjustable". The frameset on the US page is described as a special edition honouring Team Jayco AlUla in the team's colours, with the colour listed as "Howlite". It comes in six sizes, XS to XL.

## What makes the frame notable?

Giant points to several features:

- **AeroSystem Shaping.** Tube shapes developed with CFD and dynamic wind-tunnel testing. Giant says the truncated-ellipse profiles are optimised across yaw angles.
- **Advanced SL composite.** Giant's top carbon grade, woven in its own composite factory. It uses what Giant calls Continuous Fiber Technology, CNT Technology and Fusion Process.
- **Vector integrated seatpost.** Giant claims it saves weight over a standard composite post while adding aero benefit and compliance. The page gives two figures, "up to 45 grams" and "up to 40 grams", so treat the exact saving as approximate. Offset is -5/+15mm.
- **OverDrive Aero steerer.** A D-shaped fork steerer and matching spacers carry the internally routed brake and derailleur lines.
- **PowerCore bottom bracket.** An 86.5mm-wide integrated shell with asymmetric chainstays.
- **Aero bottle cages.** Separate cages for the down tube and seat tube, designed to reduce drag.

## How practical is it to live with?

Giant says the revised routing through the handlebar and frame makes cockpit changes and position adjustments quicker. That is a practical point on an aero frame whose brake and derailleur lines run internally.

The integrated seatpost is the main thing to check before buying. Giant lists a -5/+15mm offset but does not explain on the product page how saddle height is adjusted. Ask a dealer how it fits your position. Tyre clearance is 32mm and the frameset is tubeless prepared. Axles are 12x142mm at the rear and 12x100mm at the front, with disc brakes.

## What does the geometry look like?

Chainstays are 405mm and fork rake is 45mm in every size. Stack and reach follow a conventional race progression.

Giant's recommended rider heights run from 157cm on the XS to 199cm on the XL.

## What does it cost, and who is it for?

The $4,300 buys the frame, fork, integrated seatpost, computer mount and bottle cages. Groupset, wheels and cockpit are not part of the listing, so the final cost depends entirely on the build.

On warranty, Giant covers the frame for life for the original owner. Rigid forks get ten years, and paint and decals one year.

The frameset suits racers and fast group riders who want an aero frame from a brand that makes its own carbon, and who prefer to choose their own components. Riders who change position often should ask a dealer how the integrated seatpost adjusts before committing.
