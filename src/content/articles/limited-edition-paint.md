Pinarello's **Dogma F 1K** costs **€25,000**, and only 50 are made each year. That puts it about €10,500 above a standard Dogma F Dura-Ace Di2 at €14,500. Factor and Bugatti went further, on concept and on price: their **ONE** costs from **$23,599** (€25,799 in Europe), is limited to 250 numbered bikes, and is not even legal in UCI races. Below them sit a growing number of numbered runs of between 100 and 500 bikes, where the paint is the product.

## What makes a finish worth a premium?

The editions above use three approaches:

- **Exclusivity by number.** Bianchi's 113 Specialissima RC bikes mark the 113th Tour de France, where Bianchi became the official bike under a multi-year deal with organiser ASO. The livery uses the colours of the four Tour jerseys, and the bikes are built by Bianchi's Reparto Corse.
- **Less paint, not more.** Colnago's Dark Series leaves the V5Rs and Y1Rs frames as raw carbon under a minimal clear coat, inspired by Tadej Pogačar's raw-carbon Y1Rs at the 2025 Tour. Colnago sells the framesets to its Elite dealers in pairs.
- **Co-branding.** Pinarello partnered with the Swiss watchmaker Tissot for 101 Dogma F bikes, raced at Paris–Roubaix in April. Factor partnered with Bugatti for a bike with a wider fork stance, which Factor itself describes as UCI-illegal.

## What does custom paint cost without a limited run?

Trek's Project One programme is the best-documented example, but the figures we found are not current:

- In 2023 Trek charged about **$1,000–1,200** extra for its Designer Series finishes (BikeRadar).

We could not confirm current 2026 surcharges from Trek, Specialized, Cervélo, Colnago or Pinarello.

## Is a limited edition an investment?

Probably not. Run sizes of 100–500 bikes are not small for a carbon frame that will be obsolete within one or two generations. Specialized has since replaced the Tarmac SL8 with the SL9. Buy a limited edition because you like it, not for its resale value.

*Run sizes and prices come from the brands or launch coverage in Escape Collective, BikeRadar, road.cc, TOUR, BIKE, Brújula and Cyclonline. Where a brand published no price, we list it as TBC.*
