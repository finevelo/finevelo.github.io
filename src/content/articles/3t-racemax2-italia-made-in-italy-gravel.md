The Racemax² is one of the gravel bikes 3T now builds at its own carbon facility in Presezzo, Italy. The brand has named a Racemax² ITALIA_X specifically. The product page gives no price or weight, so both are TBC here. That matters for a bike at this level, and we come back to it below.

## What is the Racemax² Italia?

It is a carbon gravel bike from 3T's aero gravel range. The brand pitches that range as bikes for dirt roads and "challenging trails" that keep their pace when the surface turns to tarmac. 3T calls the Racemax² and the Extrema its "most advanced gravel bikes".

The Racemax² name also turns up beyond the bike itself. 3T sells apparel with "Racemax2-inspired" graphics.

## What does "ITALIA" actually mean?

This is the part that sets it apart. 3T says that since 2018 it has brought carbon frame production back to Italy, restarting manufacturing at Presezzo. The company says the site now turns out a full line-up of carbon frames and complete bikes.

According to 3T, the Italian operation covers the whole chain, "from design through production". The brand says it spans limited editions as well as full-series models, and both gravel and road bikes. In other words, 3T presents ITALIA as a statement about where and how the frame is made, not a paint scheme.

## How does it relate to ITALIA_X and the Extrema?

3T says it offers its Italian-built bikes in two versions, ITALIA and ITALIA_X. It has shown ITALIA_X versions of the Racemax² and the Extrema. The brand has also published a guide for riders choosing between the Racemax² ITALIA_X and the Extrema ITALIA_X.

What the homepage does not do is spell out how ITALIA and ITALIA_X differ in construction, weight or price. Nor does it list a non-X Racemax² ITALIA by name. We will not guess.

## How much does it cost, and what does it weigh?

Both are TBC. 3T's homepage lists prices for its merchandise but not for the bike, and it gives no claimed frame or complete-bike weight. If you are interested, a 3T dealer is the place to get a current quote, and 3T runs a dealer locator for this. Ask specifically which version, ITALIA or ITALIA_X, you are being quoted.

## Who is it for?

On 3T's own description, it is for riders who want one drop-bar bike that is quick on the road and capable off it. The brand frames its Italian bikes as "built for racing and adventure". It also backs gravel racing directly, through a programme that puts privateers and teams on 3T bikes and through links with the Reverb Cycling collective and the Jeroboam Gravel Series.

The less obvious buyer is the rider who cares where the frame comes from. If Italian manufacturing and a short line from designer to factory floor matter to you, that is what 3T is offering here.

## What we still don't know

Price, weight, build options, tyre clearance and geometry are not on the page we were given, and we have not ridden the bike. Until 3T publishes those figures, what we can say rests on two things: how 3T describes the bike and where it is made.
