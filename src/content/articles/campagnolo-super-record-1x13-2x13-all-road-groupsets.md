Campagnolo sells its Super Record 13-speed groupset in two configurations: the **Super Record 1x13 Road**, priced from **€3,410** for the complete groupset, and the **Super Record 2x13 All Road**, from **€4,370**. Neither product page gives a groupset weight, so weight is TBC, as are US dollar prices.

Both share one central feature: a rear derailleur with what Campagnolo calls Nano Clutch technology. The rest of the package is where they part ways.

## What are the two groupsets?

The **1x13 Road** pairs a single chainring with the Super Record 13 rear derailleur. Campagnolo positions it as a road group for riders after "performance, precision, and style", not as an off-road product.

The **2x13 All Road** adds a front derailleur and a double crankset. Campagnolo pitches it at riders who move between road and off-road terrain and do not want to compromise on performance.

## What does Nano Clutch do?

Campagnolo says that on the 1x13 Road, Nano Clutch keeps the chain "perfectly tensioned" at all times. On the 2x13 All Road, it says the system minimises the risk of chain drops.

These pages give no technical detail on the mechanism, such as clutch force or adjustability.

## What is in each groupset?

The product pages list the following components. Each page indicates 14 products, and only 12 are shown in each listing:

| Component | Super Record 1x13 Road | Super Record 2x13 All Road |
|---|---|---|
| Rear derailleur | Super Record 13 with Nano Clutch; Super Record X also listed | Super Record 13 with Nano Clutch |
| Front derailleur | – | Super Record 13 |
| Crankset | 1x Super Record, with or without power meter | Super Record 13, with or without power meter |
| Chainring | – | Ultra Chainring |
| Cassette | Super Record 13 sprockets, Super Record X sprocket set, Ultra cassette | Super Record 13 sprockets, Ultra cassette |
| Shifters | Super Record 13 Ergopower; left Ergopower control (brake lever) | Super Record 13 Ergopower; Ergopower satellite controls |
| Chain | Super Record 13 | Super Record 13 |
| Brakes | Super Record 13 caliper, Campagnolo Rotor 03 | Super Record 13 caliper, Campagnolo Rotor 03 |

Two details stand out:

- The 1x group lists a left Ergopower control described as a brake lever. With no front derailleur, a brake-only left lever would be the logical choice, though Campagnolo does not explain it on the page.
- The All Road group lists Ergopower satellite controls. Campagnolo does not describe their function or placement here.

## Who is each one for?

The **1x13 Road** is aimed at riders who want a single ring on a road bike, with a clutch to keep the chain tensioned.

The **2x13 All Road** is aimed at riders whose routes mix road and off-road terrain and who want a double crankset with a front derailleur.
