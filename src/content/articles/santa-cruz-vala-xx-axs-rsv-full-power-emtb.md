Santa Cruz lists the Vala XX AXS RSV at $13,899, the most expensive 2026 build of its full-power e-MTB. The company gives a claimed weight of 21.13 kg (46.6 lb) but does not say which frame size that refers to. The money buys the CC carbon frame, Fox Factory suspension, SRAM's XX Eagle AXS T-Type drivetrain, Reserve 30|HD carbon wheels and Bosch's Performance CX motor.

## What is the Vala?

Santa Cruz describes it as a "full-power ebike" aimed at riders who want "focused performance" on demanding trails. It runs 160 mm of fork travel and 150 mm at the rear on a four-bar suspension layout, with mixed wheels: 29in front, 27.5in rear.

The Bosch Performance CX motor delivers up to 120 Nm of torque, according to Santa Cruz, and sits low in the frame. The bike is pedal-assist only: class 1 in North America with a 20 mph limit, 25 km/h in Europe.

The 2027 Lite version is the relevant alternative. Santa Cruz says its Fox 36 fork and lighter wheels and tyres save up to a pound and a half, for $100 more. The XX AXS RSV keeps the heavier-duty Fox 38. Aluminium Valas start well below our price line.

## Why 600 Wh and not more?

Santa Cruz argues that 600 Wh suits most rides and that the lower mass helps handling. The 800 Wh Bosch battery physically does not fit. For longer days, Bosch's 250 Wh PowerMore extender bolts to the front set of down-tube bosses.

Santa Cruz says its 2027 e-bikes ship with the extender. For a limited time, buyers of qualifying 2026 carbon Valas in the US or Canada who join the Owners Club receive one free.

Charging happens in the frame: roughly 1.7 hours to 50% and 4.5 hours to full, Santa Cruz says. The system is part of Bosch's Smart System and works with the Flow app. Garmin, Wahoo and Hammerhead head units cannot display Bosch data.

## How adjustable is the geometry?

Two independent flip chips set the geometry and the suspension rate separately. The geo chip moves the bottom bracket by 4 mm and the head angle by 0.3°. The progression chip varies progression between 26% and 29%.

| BB height | 344 mm | 340 mm |

The carbon frame comes in five sizes, S to XXL, covering riders from 154 cm to 200 cm. Rear-centre length grows with size, from 439 mm to 450 mm. Santa Cruz achieves this by moving pivot locations in each front triangle rather than using different swingarms.

## What can owners change?

The frame accepts coil shocks and large air-can shocks such as the RockShox Vivid Air and Fox X2 Air. Chainrings from 32 to 36 teeth fit, though Santa Cruz says the suspension is designed around a 34. The rear brake mount takes a 200 mm rotor as standard and up to 220 mm. The bike ships with 160 mm cranks.

## Who is it for?

The Vala XX AXS RSV suits riders who want a full-power e-MTB with top-tier parts throughout and who spend time on steep ground. Santa Cruz says the mixed-wheel layout makes it easier to unweight the front on steep descents. Riders who prioritise lift-over weight over the sturdier Fox 38 may find the 2027 Lite build the better match at almost the same price.

Santa Cruz backs the frame with a lifetime warranty and free pivot-bearing replacement for the original owner.
