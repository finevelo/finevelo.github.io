Pivot Cycles has a carbon road race bike called the Pravia. On the pages we reviewed, the only build with a published price is the Pravia Force 2x AXS at **$8,499**. Pivot has not stated a weight, so that figure is **TBC**.

Pivot's own tagline is "a race bike built for humans". The pitch is a frame with race-level stiffness, paired with geometry built around positions real riders can hold for hours, not just a few minutes.

## What is Pivot promising?

Pivot says the Pravia draws on its carbon frame experience and on Grand Tour bike design. The goal is the acceleration and precision of a race bike. Pivot's Chris Cocalis frames it as refusing the idea that riders must choose between a "brutally aggressive" position and a fast bike.

Pivot says the geometry is designed around positions riders can sustain for two, three or five hours. It also says each size has its own frame shapes and carbon layup, tuned to that rider's proportions. The aim is consistent ride quality across the size range.

## How is the frame made?

Pivot says it designed the frame with Finite Element Analysis and builds it using its Hollow Core Molding process. The brand says this lets it optimise every axis of the frame. The pages we reviewed do not include stiffness or aero figures to back that up.

## What are the key details?

- **D-shaped seatpost:** Pivot says its proprietary post is shaped to cut through the wind. It also has tuned flex for comfort when seated. There is no exposed collar or bolt.
- **Virtual zero-offset seatpost position:** Pivot says this helps with fit and creates extra tyre clearance.
- **Tyre clearance:** The bike ships on 700x32c tyres. Pivot says it clears up to 700x38c with a 2x drivetrain and 700x40c with a 1x drivetrain.
- **In-frame storage:** There is room to carry spares inside the frame rather than in jersey pockets or on straps.
- **Bar and stem choice:** On Team and Pro models, buyers choose their one-piece bar/stem size when they order.

Pivot says the tyre clearance is meant to bring rough pavement, broken roads and mixed-surface routes within the bike's brief.
