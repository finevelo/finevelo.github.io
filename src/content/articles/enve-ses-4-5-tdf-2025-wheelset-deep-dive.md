ENVE's SES 4.5 is a disc-brake carbon road wheelset priced at $1,282.50 for the front and $1,567.50 for the rear, so $2,850 for the pair. ENVE gives a claimed average weight of 1,432g (±3%). Its detailed weight table lists 1,493g with tubeless tape and valves fitted, which is the more useful figure for anyone comparing ready-to-ride wheels.

A note on the name. ENVE's product page sits at a "TdF 2025" web address, but the text we drew on describes the standard SES 4.5 and gives no Tour-specific details. Anything particular to a Tour de France 2025 edition, such as finish or decals, is TBC.

## What is the SES 4.5, and where did it come from?

ENVE says the wheel began as the SES 4.5 AR in 2016. It was built to give an aero advantage on the cobbles of Paris-Roubaix while running high-volume 28-32mm tyres. ENVE calls it "the first of its kind".

The current version dates from a 2022 redesign. ENVE says it kept the tyre-optimised rim shape and added anti-pinch-flat technology. It also moved to a lighter laminate, which ENVE says improves ride feel.

Both wheels are 700c with Centerlock disc mounts. Lockrings are not included.

## Why are the front and rear rims different?

ENVE designs the two rims separately. The shallower front is shaped mainly for crosswind stability. The deeper rear is meant to recapture airflow coming off the front of the bike to cut drag.

ENVE says the SES range was developed around its own 27mm SES tyre, with testing carried out at two speeds: 32 kph (20 mph) and 48 kph (30 mph). The company emphasises weighted average drag, citing anemometer data showing that most riding happens at under 12 degrees of yaw. ENVE claims the SES 4.5 is faster than "nearly all" of its competitors' deeper wheels. That is the manufacturer's claim; we have not tested it.

## What are the tyre rules?

They are strict. The SES 4.5 is tubeless-only and uses a hookless bead.

- Aero-optimised tyre size: 27-28mm
- Minimum labelled tyre size: 27mm, and the tyre must be listed on ENVE's compatibility page
- Maximum pressure with the minimum tyre: 80 psi / 5.5 bar
- Tubeless tape width: 29mm

ENVE's patent-pending Wide Hookless Bead gives the leading edge of the rim a more forgiving surface. ENVE says this reduces the chance of pinch-flatting a tubeless tyre on impact.

## What about the hub?

The wheels use ENVE's INNERDRIVE Premium straight-pull hub. Its oversized steel ratchets sit inboard, over the bearings. ENVE says this reduces stress on the axle and bearings. ENVE claims the hub saves 60g over its previous-generation hub.

Other hub details, per ENVE:

- Interchangeable ratchets in 40, 60, 80 and 100 tooth versions
- A single spoke length for any given rim diameter, which simplifies servicing
- An anti-twist spoke hole
- Spacing between spokes that ENVE says prevents spoke creak

Freehub options are HG, XDR, N3W and Microspline. Decals come in black or white. Custom decals are available through Ground Keeper.

## Who is it for?

ENVE positions the SES 4.5 as a race wheel for speed across varied terrain. It names race days, undulating roads and technical courses with imperfect pavement. In the view of Jake Pantone, ENVE's VP of product and brand, no other wheel in the company's line-up strikes a better compromise between speed, stability, weight and versatility.

The tyre constraints matter as much as the price. If you want to run tubes or tyres narrower than 27mm, this wheelset is not compatible. It is specified for 27-28mm tubeless tyres on a 25mm internal rim.
